About K.Z. Pendrake

K.Z. Pendrake is a pen name for a contemporary author specialising in humorous fantasy and comic science fiction.

Whether it's a bureaucratic dragon demanding proper paperwork before terrorising a village, or an interstellar crew whose biggest threat is their own coffee machine, Pendrake's work finds the absurdity lurking just beneath the surface of the extraordinary.

The stories blend sharp wit with genuine heart, because the best comedy, after all, comes from characters who care deeply about things that are slightly ridiculous.
