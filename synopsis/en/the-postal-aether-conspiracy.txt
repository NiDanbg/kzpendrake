In Quibbletown, a lost letter is an annoyance. A completely collapsed postal system is a catastrophe that could end the city forever.
Following the explosive events of the magical gardening competition, Quibbletown has finally found a moment of peace. Until the mail arrives.
Love letters are singing to trolls, tax bills are materializing inside statues, and the powerful Guild of Postmen is in absolute chaos. Someone has hacked into the Postal Aether — the hidden magical dimension where every document, contract, and fine is processed — and is attempting to seize control of the city's entire bureaucracy.
Now, officially registered as a "Chaotic Mage," Wilfred "Willy" Spellburn is conscripted by the terrifying Senior Inquisitor Mrs. Plick to fix the system. Along with his cynical partner Mira, an arrogant pigeon, and a baby dragon that finds magical contracts delicious, Willy must journey deep into the Forbidden Archives and beyond reality itself.

Waiting for them in the Aether is Willy's old nemesis, Inquisitor Grumble. But Grumble isn't working alone. A far older, more dangerous entity — a forgotten archivist known only as Pale Feather — is pulling the strings, threatening to erase Quibbletown's memory entirely.

A brilliantly absurd, fast-paced fantasy adventure filled with magical red tape, origami geese, and a masterclass in bureaucratic warfare. Perfect for fans of witty, cozy, and hilarious magical satire.
