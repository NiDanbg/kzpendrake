What happens when a simple spell to fix city traffic unleashes a troll, a chocolate fountain, and a very confused dragon? Absolute disaster.
Wilfred "Willy" Spellburn isn't a bad spellcaster. He’s just exceptionally gifted at causing chaos. When his attempt to magically optimize Quibbletown's public parking goes horribly wrong, he accidentally shatters the city’s space-time barrier.
Now, he has exactly three days to fix the mess.
If he fails? The city’s terrifying Parking Inquisitor will sentence his soul to an eternity of filling out self-replicating paperwork in triplicate.
Armed with a wand that smells like burnt toast, a highly sarcastic pigeon, a street-smart witch, and a baby dragon that eats bureaucratic forms, Willy must close the interdimensional portals before his hometown is swallowed by the Rocky Void.
A laugh-out-loud tale of magical red tape, stubborn infrastructure, and spells gone delightfully wrong. Perfect for fans of witty, lighthearted, and absurdly charming fantasy.
